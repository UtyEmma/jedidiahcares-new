# Policy content notes

Prepared on 14 October 2026 for the Jedidiah Cares website. The project owner specified `info@jedidiahcares.org` as the contact for all four pages.

The copy is stored in `resources/js/data/policies.json` and displayed on `/privacy`, `/safeguarding`, `/donation-terms` and `/accessibility`. The terms page covers both website use and giving; its existing URL is retained. Publication flags enable the copy in the application. The updated date records this content revision; organisational or legal review dates have not been recorded. Global search indexing settings remain unchanged.

## Basis of the wording

- Privacy wording reflects the current email composer, website session/security cookies, locally served fonts/images and absence of configured advertising or analytics tools. The enquiry form prepares an email rather than submitting to a server inbox. Giving is disabled until a verified channel is configured. These points were checked in the application source.
- Privacy rights and lawful-processing language were informed by the [Nigeria Data Protection Commission's Act resource](https://ndpc.gov.ng/download/nigeria-data-protection-act-2023) and its [data subject rights form](https://forms.ndpc.gov.ng/dsar-request/). Rights are described subject to their applicable conditions.
- Safeguarding wording sets expectations for participant care, boundaries, activity planning, image use and reporting. The [Keeping Children Safe standards](https://www.keepingchildrensafe.global/international-child-safeguarding-standards/) informed the emphasis on prevention, responsibilities, reporting and accountability. The copy makes no claim of certification or completed background checks.
- Accessibility wording follows the user-oriented approach in [W3C's statement guidance](https://www.w3.org/WAI/planning/statements/). WCAG 2.2 AA is presented as a design goal, and the incomplete full assessment is stated. The feature descriptions reflect the website source and selected earlier manual checks.

## Points to confirm before launch

The organisation should review the commitments against its actual operations, including retention arrangements, service providers and international data transfers; participant/guardian permissions; who receives safeguarding reports and how independent escalation works; and agreed giving, restricted-gift and refund procedures. The copy uses the public email address and avoids invented officers, fixed response deadlines, refund periods, tax-relief guarantees and certification claims.

Revisit the privacy notice if server-side enquiry submission, mailing lists, analytics or payment collection are introduced. Revisit the accessibility statement after a complete assessment or substantial changes to content and interaction.
